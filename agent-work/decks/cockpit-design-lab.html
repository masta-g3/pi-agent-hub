Inspection
① Activation

Success is one complete loop: create, work in Pi, notice an explicit request, open it, and return.

② Minimum

Teach explicit NEEDS YOU, Enter to reach, Alt+Q to return, and ? or : for discovery.

③ Retirement

First-run guidance disappears after the first acknowledged return. It never becomes dashboard furniture.

④ Release

A version-scoped cue names changed daily actions below real attention. It opens no startup modal.

⑤ Boundary

No tour, wizard, demo session, new key, per-tip counter, or second help architecture.
